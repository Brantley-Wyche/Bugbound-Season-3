## Design specificity
Restrained, readable, costume-free. Family constants carry most of the identity; the honest agent-brief builder is the one Season 3 signature. The practice page (slogan headline + CTA, filled suggestion band, count-badge tabs, footer tagline) and the blur-cascade button read as generic dark dev-tool. "Engineering Lab" is a header label, not a structure. The lab records runs, passes, hints and last-worked time but shows none of it: fewer records than the desk.
