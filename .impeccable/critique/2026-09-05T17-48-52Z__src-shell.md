## Cognitive load and emotional journey

The four section actions and three hint tiers are manageable. The 12-entry Core React register exceeds four visible items, but it is an ordered curriculum with a recommended current assignment and sequential locks, not twelve equal immediate decisions. No artificial regrouping is recommended.
